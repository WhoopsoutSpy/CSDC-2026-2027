In the Drone Jamming Attack and Defense challenge, teams compete against each other in a red-team blue-team jamming and anti-jamming competition.

The goal of the red team is to stop the blue team's drone from reaching its goal by jamming it. The red team must be able to autonomously detect the drone during flight and send a jamming signal. The jammer must only jam when the drone is detected and there must be a way to prove the drone was detected for judges to verify.

The goal of the blue team is to avoid being jammed so that their drone can reach its goal. The blue team can employ a variety of tactics to avoid jamming, so long as the method is non-destructive (cannot physically break the jammer).

The red team will be scored on the ability to detect the drone and the result of the jamming, while the blue team will be scored on the ability of the drone to complete its goal and protect against the jamming.
