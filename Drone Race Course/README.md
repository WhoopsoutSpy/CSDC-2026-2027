The Drone Race Course is all about flying from gate to gate as fast as possible. 

In this challenge, students must have their drone fly through all gates in numerical order, fully autonomously. Once the drone takes off, it must find the correct gate, fly to its center, and then move on to the next gate until the entire course has been completed. 

Unlike previous challenges, multiple teams will be competing against each other at the SAME TIME. This presents a unique situation, in which teams must find a way to both navigate the course quickly and avoid other drones at the sme time.

Teams will be scored both on their ability to get through the course AND on their ability to avoid other drones. While the exact rubric is not available currently, we will provide a breakdown in the near future.
