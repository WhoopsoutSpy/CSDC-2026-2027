The Drone Swarm Attack challenge puts teams into the headspace of attackers, in which they must detect drones on a network, take control over them, and make them perform alternative movements.

Judges will have two or more drones on a shared network taking commands and acting as a swarm. Teams must get onto this network, identify which drone is which, and perform an ARP spoofing attack on a drone to shift control away from the swarm. Once the team successfully takes control of the drone, they must make the drone perform movements that are different from the swarm to visually illustrate their control of the drone.

Teams must do this for all drones within the swarm to show the reproducibility of the attack. Grading will be done on the team's ability to take control of the drones in the swarm.
