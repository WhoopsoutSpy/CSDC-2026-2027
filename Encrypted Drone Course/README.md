The Encrypted Drone Course increases the complexity compared to the Drone Race Course through the inclusion of encrypted QR codes that the drone must decrypt and act upon.

Students must get their drone to navigate the course fully autonomously, moving from gate to gate in numerical order. While the drone is flying to its next gate, it must scan and decrypt any encrypted QR codes it finds and validate which are legitimate and illegitimate. 

Once the drone gets to the center of the gate, it must perform the actions from ONLY the legitimate QR code it scanned and NOT from any of the illegitimate QR codes.

After the drone completes the encrypted QR code movements, it will then move on to the next gate until the entire course has been completed.

Teams will be scored both on their ability to complete the course as well as the ability of their drone to act on legitimate QR code movement commands.
